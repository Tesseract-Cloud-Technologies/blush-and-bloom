About Blush & Bloom

Beauty that celebrates every shade, every skin type, every story.

Home / About
"Beauty shouldn't cost the earth, or your health."

Started in a spare bedroom. Built for everyone.

Blush & Bloom began in 2021 when our founder couldn't find clean, affordable makeup made for a wide range of skin tones locally. What started as a small batch of lipsticks sold from a spare bedroom has grown into a full beauty line, still formulated with the same values.

Every product is cruelty-free, most are vegan, and we test shade ranges on real customers before anything goes on sale.
