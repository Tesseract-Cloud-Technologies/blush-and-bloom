Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?

Orders within Papua New Guinea typically arrive in 3-5 business days. You'll receive tracking details once your order ships.

Are your products cruelty-free?

Yes, every Blush & Bloom product is cruelty-free and most are also fully vegan. Look for the leaf icon on individual product pages.

Can I return a product?

Unopened products can be returned within 14 days of delivery. Reach out to our team and we'll walk you through it.

Do you ship outside PNG?

Not yet, but it's on our roadmap! For now we ship island-wide within Papua New Guinea.
